Natural Computing Rubric.

B Methodology/experimental design

This rubric assesses reports on key criteria (problem definition, methodology, analysis, conclusions,
and report), which are outlined in Sections A-E. They are related to the course learning objectives:
After the course, you can, given natural computing methods from the course or scientific literature:
LO1 … explain how these methods work and why one would use them.
LO2 … implement, assess, and further develop these methods to solve a learning task
or simulate a biological system.
LO3 … design, analyze, and interpret experiments applying these methods to a specific
question and/or dataset.
LO4 … communicate a problem statement, methodology, experimental outcomes and
conclusions through appropriate visual and/or verbal means in a research report
meeting scientific standards in this field.
